About

Born in Southern Louisiana and forged in Los Angeles, Rowdy Hop is a country, rock, and hip hop crossover act that pairs southern grit with West Coast swagger. Big hooks, real-life storytelling, and main-stage energy turn every set into a party.

The story. Rowdy Hop is Tyler Forrest's own journey from underdog to his best self, and Rowdy the racehorse is the symbol of that run. The debut album Stable to Stage started the ride, and the next album, Fire Horse, goes deeper into transformation, heartbreak and comeback.
